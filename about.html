Our Story

Emilia's Place is a family-owned private resort located in Sapalibutad, Angeles City. We created this resort to provide a peaceful and exclusive place where families and friends can relax, celebrate special occasions, and enjoy quality time together away from the busy city.

  • Family-Owned & Managed
  • Located in Sapalibutad, Angeles City
  • Peaceful & Accessible Location

What Makes Us Different?

We offer exclusive full-house rental, giving every group complete privacy during their stay. Whether you're planning a family gathering, a celebration, or a simple weekend getaway, you can enjoy the resort without sharing the space with other guests.

  • 100% Exclusive Full-House Rental
  • No Shared Spaces with Other Groups
  • Customizable Event Setups
